## Governance

cronoq follows a simple maintainer model:

- Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, and cut releases.
- Decisions are made by consensus in issues and pull requests. Changes to scheduling semantics,
  public APIs, the wire protocol or the store schema need an issue or discussion first and
  approval from a maintainer.
- Contributors who make sustained, high-quality contributions may be invited to become
  maintainers.
- Security reports are handled privately by the maintainers as described in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
